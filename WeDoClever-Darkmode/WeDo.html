FAQ

Common questions

How long does a typical web project take?+
A standard 5–10 page website typically takes 4–6 weeks from discovery to launch. Complex platforms or e-commerce sites run 8–14 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline before any work begins.
Do you work with clients outside India?+
Yes! We work with clients globally. Our team is experienced with async collaboration across time zones. Clients in the US, UK, UAE, and Southeast Asia are among our regulars.
How soon can I expect SEO results?+
Technical SEO improvements can show measurable impact within 4–8 weeks. Significant ranking increases for competitive keywords typically take 3–6 months. We set realistic expectations and report transparently.
Can you maintain and update my site after launch?+
Absolutely. We offer flexible maintenance retainers covering security updates, performance monitoring, content changes, and feature additions — often bundled with our SEO retainer.
Do you offer a Contact Now?+
Yes — a complimentary website and SEO audit for businesses with an existing online presence. Covers Core Web Vitals, technical SEO, mobile-friendliness, and quick-win opportunities. No commitment required.
