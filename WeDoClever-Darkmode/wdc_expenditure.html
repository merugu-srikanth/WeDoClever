💡 Key Advice for Early Months

📌 Register as LLP first
Cheaper to run than Pvt Ltd. Upgrade only when clients require it. Saves ₹5k–₹10k in setup costs.
📌 Focus on SEO retainers
They give predictable income every month unlike one-time web projects. Target 5–8 retainer clients in year one.
📌 Get case study clients fast
Do 2–3 discounted or free projects for local businesses — testimonials and case studies will close your future deals automatically.
📌 Work from home for 6 months
Saves ₹8k–₹15k/month on rent. Reinvest that into digital ads to get more clients. Office can wait.
